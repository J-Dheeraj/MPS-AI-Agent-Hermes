# MOM / Employment Policy Reference — MPS Casework (2025/2026)

## Work pass types — overview

| Pass | Who it's for | Min salary (2025/2026) | Quota |
|---|---|---|---|
| Employment Pass (EP) | Professionals, managers, executives | $5,600/month (from Sep 2025) | No quota |
| S Pass | Mid-skilled workers | $3,150/month | Quota applies |
| Work Permit (WP) | Semi-skilled, blue-collar | Sector-specific | Quota applies |
| Dependant's Pass (DP) | Spouse/children of EP/S Pass holders | N/A | No quota |
| Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP) | Foreign spouses of citizens/PRs; others | N/A | No quota |

---

## Employment Pass (EP)

- **Minimum salary:** $5,600/month from September 2025 (higher for financial services sector: $6,200/month)
- **COMPASS framework:** Points-based assessment since 2023 — evaluates salary, qualifications, diversity, firm support for locals
- **Common MPS case:** EP renewal rejected or downgraded to S Pass
  **Action:** MP letter to MOM Controller of Work Passes requesting review; attach employer's justification letter and employee's credentials. Note: MOM has wide discretion; MP letter signals the case but does not guarantee reconsideration.

---

## S Pass

- **Minimum salary:** $3,150/month
- **Subject to quota:** Each employer has an S Pass quota based on total workforce size
- **Common MPS case:** S Pass rejected despite meeting salary criteria
  Possible cause: employer has hit quota ceiling
  **Action:** Explain quota constraint; employer must reduce other S Pass holders or hire more locals to create headroom. MP letter to MOM for exceptional cases citing business need.

---

## Dependant's Pass (DP)

- **For:** Legally married spouses and unmarried children under 21 of EP holders (minimum $6,000/month) or S Pass holders (minimum $6,000/month)
- **Working on DP:** DP holders need a Letter of Consent (LOC) to work
- **Common MPS case:** Spouse on DP wants to work
  **Action:** Employer must apply for LOC. Direct to MOM website. LOC is employer-specific — tied to the job, not the person.

## Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP) and LTVP+

### LTVP — for foreign spouses of Singapore citizens

- **Sponsor income requirement:** Citizen sponsor must earn ≥$2,500/month (PR sponsors face higher scrutiny)
- **Duration:** Typically 1–2 years; renewable
- **LTVP+:** Granted to spouses with longer stays and demonstrably closer ties (children born here, long-term employment history, community integration)
- **Working on LTVP:** LTVP holders need a **Letter of Consent (LOC)** from MOM to work legally. Employer applies.

### Common MPS cases — foreign spouse

**1. LTVP rejected**
Most common cause: sponsor income below $2,500/month, or ICA/MOM not satisfied with genuineness of marriage/relationship.
**Action:** MP letter to ICA (not MOM) for LTVP — it is an ICA-issued pass. Focus on: length of relationship, children, community ties, sponsor stability. *See SKILL-ica for full ICA guidance.*

**2. LTVP+ not granted despite long stay**
**Action:** MP letter to ICA requesting LTVP+ upgrade; cite years of residence, children's birth/schooling in Singapore, employment history.

**3. Foreign spouse on LTVP cannot find work**
LTVP holders are not automatically allowed to work. Employer must apply for LOC. If no employer will apply: no legal work route under LTVP. Advise constituent to explore whether PR application is appropriate.
**Action:** Explain LOC requirement; MP letter to MOM for LOC if employer exists but application is pending.

## Salary disputes and non-payment

### TADM (Tripartite Alliance for Dispute Management)

- **First stop** for all salary and employment disputes
- Provides mediation between employer and employee
- Free for employees
- Contact: tadm.sg or call 1800-221-9088
- **Timeline:** Mediation typically within 2–4 weeks of filing

### Employment Claims Tribunal (ECT)

- If TADM mediation fails, claim goes to ECT
- Handles salary, notice pay, wrongful dismissal (for certain categories)
- **Claim limit:** $20,000 (or $30,000 with TADM's consent)

### MP role in salary disputes

- MP letter to MOM is appropriate when:
  - Employer is ignoring TADM process
  - Employer has closed down / absconded
  - Worker is a vulnerable foreign worker unable to navigate the system
- MP letter should request MOM to investigate and take enforcement action

## Retrenchment

### What employers must do

- Notify MOM if retrenching ≥10 employees within 30 days
- Give retrenchment benefits per collective agreement or contract (no statutory minimum, but **norm is 2 weeks per year of service**)
- Issue a certificate of employment within 3 days if requested

### Common MPS cases

**Retrenchment without benefits**
**Action:** TADM first; MP letter to MOM if employer is uncooperative. State years of service, expected benefit amount, and what employer has refused.

**No notice or insufficient notice**
**Action:** Entitled to salary-in-lieu of notice per contract. Refer to TADM; MP letter if employer ignores.

## Workfare Income Supplement (WIS)

- **Eligibility:** Singapore citizen, age 30+ (35+ for persons with disabilities), gross monthly income $500–$3,000, working (employee or self-employed)
- **Paid:** Quarterly, automatically via PayNow/bank — no application needed if employer submits CPF contributions
- **Self-employed:** Must declare income to IRAS annually and contribute to MediSave to receive WIS
- **Common MPS case:** Not receiving WIS despite appearing eligible
  Possible causes: employer not submitting CPF, income outside range, self-employed constituent not declaring to IRAS
  **Action:** Check CPF contribution history; MP letter to MOM/CPF if employer is failing to contribute

## Workplace injury

- Covered under Work Injury Compensation Act (WICA)
- **Who is covered:** All employees (including foreign workers)
- **File claim:** At MOM within 1 year of accident; employer's insurer pays
- **Common MPS case:** Employer disputing injury claim or delaying payment
  **Action:** MP letter to MOM requesting investigation; constituent should also engage a lawyer if injury is severe

## Common MPS case types

| Case | Recommended action |
|---|---|
| EP renewal rejected | MP letter to MOM; employer to attach justification |
| Foreign spouse cannot work (LTVP) | Explain LOC; employer must apply |
| Salary not paid | TADM first; MP letter if employer ignores |
| Retrenched without benefits | TADM first; MP letter to MOM if unresolved |
| Workfare not received | Check CPF submissions; MP letter if employer issue |
| Workplace injury — employer disputing | MP letter to MOM; advise legal representation |
| S Pass quota full | Explain quota; employer must adjust workforce ratio |
| LTVP rejected — foreign spouse | MP letter to ICA (not MOM) — see SKILL-ica |
