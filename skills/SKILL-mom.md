## Work passes — key thresholds (2025/2026)

### Employment Pass (EP)

- **Minimum salary:** **$5,600/month** (from September 2025)
- For higher-skilled professionals, managers, executives; degree-level qualifications expected
- Assessed under COMPASS framework: points-based assessment including salary, qualifications, company diversity
- EP holders may bring dependants (LTVP, DP) if earning above a threshold

### S Pass

- **Minimum salary:** **$3,150/month**
- Mid-skilled workers; quota applies (up to 10% of workforce in most sectors)
- Subcontractors: stricter quota (8%)

### Work Permit (WP)

- For lower-skilled workers; sector-specific (construction, marine, domestic)
- No minimum salary fixed nationally but sector-specific guidelines apply
- Levy applies to employer

### Dependant's Pass (DP) and Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP)

- DP: for legally married spouse and unmarried children under 21 of EP / S Pass holders
- LTVP: for parents, parents-in-law, common-law spouse, step-children
- **LTVP for foreign spouse of Singapore citizen:** sponsor must earn ≥**$2,500/month**
- LTVP+: for those who have stayed longer and have demonstrably closer ties to Singapore

### Letter of Consent (LOC)

- Allows DP holder to work in Singapore without a separate work pass
- Employer must apply; no minimum salary requirement

## Employment disputes

### TADM (Tripartite Alliance for Dispute Management)

- **First stop for:** unpaid salary, wrongful dismissal, unfair retrenchment, employment contract disputes
- Process: file claim online at tadm.gov.sg → mediation appointment → settlement or referral to ECT
- Time limit: 1 year from the date of the dispute (salary claims), or 1 month from dismissal (unfair dismissal)
- **MP's role:** A letter is appropriate if TADM has been unresponsive, if the constituent faces language barriers, or if there is urgency (imminent repatriation of foreign worker, etc.)

### Salary disputes

- Constitute must file at TADM first — MP cannot bypass this
- Write to MOM (Commissioner for Labour) if TADM process has stalled

## Retrenchment

- Employers must notify MOM if retrenching ≥10 employees in any 6-month period
- Retrenchment benefit: not mandatory by law but recommended; 2 weeks to 1 month per year of service (MOM advisory)
- Constituent who was wrongly denied retrenchment benefit: file at TADM
- MP letter: useful to flag to MOM if company is retrenching without following guidelines

## Workfare Income Supplement (WIS)

- **Who:** Singapore citizens aged 30+ (35+ for Malay/Indian/minority group residents), working in lower-wage jobs
- **What:** Cash + CPF top-up to supplement income for lower-wage workers
- **Self-employed:** also eligible but must contribute MediSave
- Administered by CPF Board; eligibility assessed annually from tax data
- If constituent believes they qualify but did not receive: write to CPF Board

## Common MPS cases

### Foreign spouse cannot get LTVP / LTVP renewal

- Check: sponsor is Singapore citizen and earns ≥$2,500/month?
- If income does not meet threshold but there are compelling reasons (children, long marriage), make the humanitarian case
- Write to: Commissioner of Immigration, Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (not MOM — LTVP is ICA for foreign spouses)

### Employer did not pay salary

- Constituent should file at TADM first
- If urgent (e.g. construction worker living on site, about to be deported), escalate urgency in letter to Commissioner for Labour, MOM

### EP / S Pass rejected

- MOM does not always give reasons for rejection
- MP letter can request MOM to reconsider, citing business need and specific qualifications
- Write to: Commissioner for Work Passes, Ministry of Manpower

### Domestic helper dispute (employer or helper)

- Write to: Commissioner for Work Permits, Ministry of Manpower
- Note: employment agencies are also regulated by MOM
