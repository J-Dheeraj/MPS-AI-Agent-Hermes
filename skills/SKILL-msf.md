# MSF / Social Assistance Policy Reference — MPS Casework (2025/2026)

## ComCare — the three tiers

### 1. Crisis / Emergency Assistance

- **For:** Immediate, acute financial crisis — eviction today, no food, acute medical emergency, family violence situation
- **What it provides:** One-off cash, vouchers, referrals to other services
- **How to access:** Walk in to nearest Social Service Office (SSO) same day; OR MP writes urgent referral letter marked "URGENT — CRISIS"
- **No strict means test:** SSO officers assess on the spot
- **MP role:** Urgent referral letter speeds up same-day response

### 2. Short-to-Medium Term Assistance (SMTA)

- **For:** Families and individuals temporarily unable to support themselves (job loss, illness, family breakdown, caregiver burden)
- **What it provides:**
  - Monthly cash assistance
  - Medical fee waivers at polyclinics and restructured hospitals
  - School meal subsidies and school fees assistance
  - Utilities assistance
- **Duration:** Typically 3–6 months, renewable based on assessment
- **How to access:** Apply at SSO; SSO conducts holistic needs assessment
- **Income guide:** Rough guide ~$1,900/month per capita or below, but assessment is holistic — not a hard cutoff
- **MP role:** MP referral letter to SSO requesting expedited assessment significantly speeds up processing. Useful for urgent situations.

### 3. Long-Term Assistance (Public Assistance — PA)

⚠️ **Note:** "PA" or "Public Assistance" is the correct term to use. Do NOT abbreviate as "LTA" — that abbreviation refers to the Land Transport Authority.

- **For:** Persons who are permanently unable to work due to age, illness, or disability, with no family support
- **What it provides:** Monthly cash assistance (~$570/month for a single person; varies by household size and circumstances)
- **How to access:** Apply at SSO; strict means test
- **MP role:** MP letter useful if application was rejected and circumstances genuinely meet criteria

---

## Key point for MP letters to MSF/SSO

MSF assessments are **holistic — not purely income-based.** An MP letter that explains the human situation (caregiver burden, sudden job loss, medical condition, family circumstances) carries weight beyond just stating income figures. The SSO officer has discretion.

Always ask the SSO to conduct an **expedited assessment** if there is genuine urgency (imminent eviction, no food, medical need).

## Silver Support Scheme

- **For:** Elderly Singaporeans in the bottom 20% of income earners who had low wages during their working years
- **Eligibility:**
  - Singapore citizen aged 65 and above
  - Living in HDB flat (1-room to 5-room)
  - Not owning private property
  - Gross personal income ≤$1,800/month in working years
- **Payout (2025/2026):**
  - 1- and 2-room flat: $360/quarter
  - 3-room flat: $270/quarter
  - 4-room flat: $180/quarter
- **Paid quarterly:** February, May, August, November via PayNow/bank
- **Common MPS issue:** Constituent did not receive payment, or was assessed as ineligible but believes they should qualify
- **MP role:** Letter to MSF requesting review of eligibility assessment; attach evidence of income history if available

## Social Service Offices (SSO)

There is one SSO in each major HDB estate. Constituents should be referred to their **nearest SSO** based on home address.

SSOs conduct holistic family assessments and connect families to:

- ComCare
- Eldercare services
- Mental health referrals
- Child development support
- Employment assistance

**For MP office:** SSO referral letters from MPs are taken seriously and often result in faster appointment scheduling.

---

## ComLink+ (family support programme)

- For families with multiple and complex needs (financial + social)
- SSO assigns a dedicated ComLink officer who coordinates multiple agencies on behalf of the family
- **MP role:** MP letter recommending family for ComLink+ assessment can trigger a more intensive support response

---

## CDC Vouchers (2026)

- Distributed annually to all Singaporean households
- Redeemable at hawker centres, heartland merchants, supermarkets
- Claimed via Singpass at go.gov.sg/cdcv
- **Common MPS issue:** Constituent did not receive / cannot claim
- **MP role:** Direct constituent to CDC website or CDC office; MP can write to CDC if technical claim issue persists

## Common MPS case types

| Case | Recommended action |
|---|---|
| No money for food / utilities today | Urgent SSO referral; mark CRISIS |
| Just lost job, family cannot cope | SSO referral for SMTA assessment |
| Elderly, no income, family not helping | SSO referral for Public Assistance (PA) / Silver Support check |
| Silver Support not received | MP letter to MSF requesting payment review |
| SSO rejected ComCare — disputed | MP letter requesting review; attach evidence |
| Complex family — multiple needs | Request ComLink+ assessment via SSO referral |
| CDC vouchers not received | Direct to CDC; escalate if technical issue |
