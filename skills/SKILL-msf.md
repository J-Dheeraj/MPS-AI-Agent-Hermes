## ComCare tiers

### Crisis / Emergency Assistance

- **Who:** Individuals and families facing sudden, urgent hardship (job loss, medical emergency, domestic crisis)
- **What:** Immediate one-off cash assistance; no fixed amount — assessed case by case
- **How:** Apply directly at any SSO; no appointment needed in genuine emergency
- **Timeline:** Same-day or next-day disbursement possible for urgent cases
- **MP's role:** A letter is useful if SSO has been unresponsive or constituent was wrongly turned away

### Short-to-Medium Term Assistance (SMTA)

- **Who:** Individuals/families with low income, going through a difficult period but expected to recover self-sufficiency
- **What:** Monthly cash assistance + medical fee waivers + education subsidies; duration typically 3–24 months
- **Assessment:** Holistic — includes household income, assets, debts, family support, willingness to work toward self-sufficiency
- **Income ceiling:** No fixed threshold — assessed holistically; typically household per capita income below $1,500
- **Apply:** Through SSO; caseworker assigned
- **Review:** Periodic — constituents must show progress toward financial independence
- **MP's role:** Useful if SMTA has been declined unfairly or constituent is struggling with the application process

### Long-Term Assistance (Public Assistance / PA)

- **Who:** Permanently incapacitated individuals with no family support and no other income source
- **What:** Monthly cash grant (amount is very small — last resort)
- **Eligibility:** Very strict means test; applicant must be unable to work; family members must be unable to support
- **Apply:** Through SSO; in-depth assessment required
- **MP's role:** Write if constituent clearly qualifies but has been denied or not assessed

## Silver Support Scheme

- **Who:** Singapore citizens aged 65+ in the bottom 20% of elderly income earners
- **What:** Quarterly cash payment into CPF accounts
  - 1- and 2-room HDB flat: **$360/quarter**
  - 3-room HDB flat: **$270/quarter**
  - 4-room HDB flat: **$180/quarter**
  - Private property or 5-room+: not eligible
- **Administered by:** CPF Board on behalf of MSF
- **Not received:** Check eligibility at cpf.gov.sg; changes in flat type or income may affect eligibility
- **MP's role:** Write to MSF if constituent appears to qualify but has not received payment

## ComLink+ (for families with young children)

- Pilot programme connecting low-income families with sustained support from community partners
- Families assigned a dedicated befriender / case manager
- Focus on: employment, education, housing, health
- Referral: through SSO or MP's office

## Social Service Offices (SSO)

- 24 SSOs islandwide — the front door for ComCare applications
- Each SSO covers a geographical area
- SSO locator: supportgowhere.life.gov.sg
- Opening hours: typically Mon–Fri 8am–6pm, some Saturdays
- Constituent must go in person for initial assessment in most cases

## Common MPS cases

### Constituent applied for ComCare and was rejected

- Confirm they applied through SSO, not a non-profit
- MP letter should: state the hardship clearly, note any exceptional circumstances, request the SSO to review the assessment
- Write to: Director, ComCare, Ministry of Social and Family Development

### Constituent urgently needs help (food, rent) tonight

- Refer to nearest SSO for Crisis Assistance — no letter needed, go now
- Also refer to: Community Development Councils (CDC), Salvation Army, Food from the Heart, depending on need
- MP's office can call the SSO to flag as urgent

### Silver Support not received

- Check eligibility criteria first (flat type, age, income)
- If eligible, write to: Head, Silver Support, Ministry of Social and Family Development (care of CPF Board)

### Elderly living alone, no family support, self-neglect concerns

- This may require MSF Senior Activity Centre involvement
- If there is a welfare / abuse concern: MSF APS (Adult Protective Service): 1800-777-0000
- If not an emergency: write to MSF to request welfare check
