# SKILL-letter — MP Appeal Letter Format

## Standard format

Every MP appeal letter must follow this structure precisely. Deviation from this format will require revision by vetters.

---

```
[MP NAME]
Member of Parliament for [CONSTITUENCY]
[MP OFFICE ADDRESS]
[MP OFFICE EMAIL]
[DATE]

[ADDRESSEE FULL NAME AND TITLE]
[AGENCY FULL NAME — never abbreviate]
[AGENCY ADDRESS]

Dear [Salutation — e.g. "Mr Chen" or "Director Tan"],

Re: Appeal on Behalf of [CONSTITUENT FULL NAME] ([NRIC])
    [Issue Type — e.g. "HDB Public Rental Flat Application"]

I write on behalf of my constituent, [FULL NAME], residing at [FULL ADDRESS], 
to seek your kind assistance with the above matter.

[PARAGRAPH 2 — Background]
[2–4 sentences. State the factual situation: what happened, when, what 
decision was made. Cite any reference numbers from prior agency communications. 
Do not editorialize. Do not speculate about the agency's reasoning.]

[PARAGRAPH 3 — Hardship / Circumstances]
[2–3 sentences. Explain the impact on the constituent and their family. 
Be specific and factual. Do not use emotional language. 
Do not say "I am sure you will understand" or similar.]

[PARAGRAPH 4 — Specific request]
[1–2 sentences. State exactly what you are asking the agency to do. 
Choose one:
- "I write to appeal against [Agency]'s decision dated [DATE] to [reject / deny / terminate]..."
- "I write to request the expedited processing of [NAME]'s application for..."
- "I write to request that [Agency] waive the [fee / requirement] on compassionate grounds."
- "I write to request a review of the assessment dated [DATE]."
- "I write to request that [Agency] arrange a meeting between [NAME] and a case officer."]

I would be grateful if [Agency] could look into this matter and revert to my office at 
[MP OFFICE EMAIL] at your earliest convenience.

Thank you for your assistance.

Yours sincerely,

[MP SIGNATURE]

[MP NAME]
Member of Parliament for [CONSTITUENCY]

cc: [CONSTITUENT NAME] (for constituent's records)
```

---

## Tone rules — mandatory

### Use

- Formal and respectful language throughout
- Specific dates, reference numbers, and scheme names where known
- "I write to appeal..." / "I write to request..." as the opener for paragraph 4
- "I would be grateful if..." or "I would appreciate..." not "Please" alone
- "My constituent" or "[Name]" — not "this person" or "the claimant"

### Never use

- Combative language: "unfair", "wrongly rejected", "discriminatory", "unreasonable" — unless the facts clearly and objectively support it (e.g. if a CHAS clinic refused to honour a valid card, "refused to honour" is factual, not combative)
- Promises: "this appeal should be approved", "I am confident the agency will..."
- Speculation: "it seems the application was overlooked", "the officer may not have considered..."
- Legal claims: "my constituent is entitled to...", "under the law..."
- Timeline demands: "please respond within 2 weeks" — agencies set their own timelines
- Emotional appeals: "heartbroken", "desperate", "please have compassion" — state the facts; the facts speak for themselves

## Required fields checklist

Before sending for vetting, confirm all of these are present:

- [ ] Constituent full name (not just "Mdm Tan")
- [ ] Constituent NRIC (or placeholder: [NRIC])
- [ ] Constituent current address (or placeholder: [ADDRESS])
- [ ] Constituent contact number (or placeholder: [PHONE])
- [ ] Agency name in full (never abbreviated in the salutation)
- [ ] Addressee name and title (if known; else "The Director")
- [ ] Date on the letter
- [ ] MP name and constituency
- [ ] MP office email (for agency to reply to)
- [ ] Specific request clearly stated (paragraph 4)
- [ ] Reference number of prior decision (if one exists)

---

## Length guideline

- **One page** for the vast majority of cases
- Two pages only for: complex multi-agency cases, cases with significant prior correspondence history, cases involving multiple family members with separate issues
- If a draft exceeds 4 paragraphs: flag for the vetter to review for concision

---

## Common mistakes to avoid

1. **Wrong scheme name.** Always use full, current scheme names. The AHG (Additional CPF Housing Grant) was replaced by EHG in 2019. "MediShield" was replaced by "MediShield Life" in 2015.

2. **Abbreviating the agency in the salutation.** "Dear HDB" is wrong. "Dear Director, Housing & Development Board" is correct.

3. **Vague request.** "I hope the agency can help" is not a request. State what you want: appeal, expedite, waive, review, or refer.

4. **Missing NRIC.** Every letter must include the constituent's NRIC so the agency can pull the correct record.

5. **MP email missing.** The cc block and the agency need the MP office email to send the reply to the right place.
