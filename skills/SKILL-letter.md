# MPS Appeal Letter Format

## Standard structure

[MP LETTERHEAD]

[MP NAME]

Member of Parliament for [CONSTITUENCY]

[DATE — DD Month YYYY]

The [Director / Chief Executive / Registrar]

[AGENCY FULL NAME]

[AGENCY ADDRESS]

Dear Sir/Madam,

Re: Appeal on Behalf of [CONSTITUENT FULL NAME] (NRIC: [NRIC])

    [ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION OF ISSUE — e.g. "Request to Review HDB Rental Flat Application"]

I write on behalf of my constituent, [NAME], who resides at [ADDRESS]
and may be contacted at [PHONE / EMAIL].

[PARAGRAPH 1 — Situation: factual, specific, no embellishment. Include
relevant dates, reference numbers, and what the constituent has already
tried or been told by the agency.]

[PARAGRAPH 2 — Request: state exactly what is being asked. Choose one:

  • appeal a decision (cite the decision date and reference number)
  • expedite a process (state the reason for urgency)
  • waive a fee or requirement (state which one)
  • seek a review of eligibility (state which scheme)
  • request a case officer meeting (state why in-person is needed)
  • refer to another agency (name the agency)

Reference the specific scheme or policy by its full name.]

[PARAGRAPH 3 — optional: mitigating circumstances, medical conditions,
family hardship, or community ties that support the appeal. Keep brief
and factual. Do not speculate or make promises.]

I would be grateful if [AGENCY] could look into this matter
favourably and update [NAME] directly at the contact details above,
copying my office at [MP OFFICE EMAIL].

Thank you for your assistance.

Yours faithfully,

[MP NAME]

Member of Parliament for [CONSTITUENCY]

## Tone rules

DO:

- Formal, respectful, factual
- Empathetic without being emotional
- Name the exact scheme and decision being appealed
- State one clear request per letter

DO NOT:

- Use "unfair", "wrongly rejected", "discriminatory" without clear facts
- Promise an outcome: never "should be approved", "entitled to", "must grant"
- Speculate about agency intent
- Include information about the constituent not given to you
- Set timelines for the agency ("please respond within 14 days")
- Exceed one page unless the case genuinely requires more

---

## Urgency language (use sparingly, only when true)

For genuine urgency, add to the opening:

> I write urgently on behalf of my constituent...

And add to the closing paragraph before sign-off:

> Given the time-sensitive nature of [NAME]'s situation — [one-sentence
> reason for urgency] — I would be most grateful for an expedited response.

Appropriate urgency reasons:

- Imminent eviction or homelessness
- Medical procedure pending financial clearance
- Visa expiry within days
- Active domestic violence situation
- Child welfare risk
