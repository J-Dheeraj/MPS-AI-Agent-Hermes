# Formal Letter Format — Singapore Government Agency

## Structure
1. Date (DD Month YYYY)
2. Addressee: The Director / Director-General, [Agency Name]
3. Subject line: bold, CAPITALS
4. Opening: "Dear Sir/Madam,"
5. Body: 2-3 short paragraphs
   - Para 1: Who the constituent is, what they are requesting
   - Para 2: Supporting reasons / grounds
   - Para 3: Request for reconsideration / assistance
6. Closing: "Yours faithfully,"
7. Signature: MP's name + constituency

## Tone
- Formal and respectful
- Objective — state facts, not opinions
- Concise — one page maximum

## What NOT to include
- Full NRIC (use last 3 digits + letter only, e.g. 567A)
- Medical details unless directly relevant
- Emotional language
- Criticisms of agency staff
